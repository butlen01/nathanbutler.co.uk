Company Case Study — Research & Marketing

Turning Research and Marketing Into Profit Centres

Two standalone cost centres, market research and marketing, treated as necessary spend with no expectation of a return. This is how both were turned into self-funding income streams instead.

Top 4 of 36 divisions ranked for cross-sell, four out of five

Situation

Market research and marketing were run as pure overhead, cost lines with no expectation of a return, at a point where every line of cost was under scrutiny. Neither function had ever been asked to pay for itself, and both were the kind of spend that gets cut first when a budget tightens.

Approach

Changed the format the research team produced, from an internal document into something a developer would pay for, and started selling it directly — up to £15k a time — with training rolled out to every BDM to support the pitch. On marketing, put a 10% levy on every piece of material produced on a developer's behalf, turning a cost too small to bill for individually into real, per-developer upside.

Result

Both functions became self-funding income streams, which protected them from the next round of budget cuts rather than making them a target for one. Four of the business's five divisions ranked in the group's top four of thirty-six for cross-sell — proof the model worked well beyond a single site or team.
