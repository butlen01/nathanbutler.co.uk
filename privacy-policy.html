Privacy Policy

This page explains what data this site collects, why, and what rights you have over it. It is short because the site itself does very little: a contact form and one privacy-focused analytics tool.

Who Controls This Data

This site is owned and operated by Nathan Butler. For any question about this policy or your data, contact butlen01@outlook.com.

What This Site Collects

Contact form. If you submit the form on the Contact page, the name, email address, organisation (if given) and message you enter are sent to Netlify, the service that hosts this site and processes its forms, and forwarded so I can read and reply to it. A hidden field on the form exists purely to catch automated spam submissions and does not collect anything about you.

Analytics. This site uses Plausible Analytics, a privacy-focused analytics tool that counts visits and page views in aggregate. It does not use cookies, does not track you across other websites, and does not collect or store any information that identifies you personally.

Nothing else. There are no advertising trackers, social media pixels, or third-party marketing tools on this site.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies.

How This Data Is Used

Contact form submissions are used only to respond to your enquiry. Analytics data is used only to understand, in aggregate, which pages are useful to visitors. Neither is sold or shared with any third party beyond the processors named above (Netlify and Plausible), which handle it only to provide their respective services to this site.

How Long Data Is Kept

Contact form submissions are kept for as long as needed to respond to you, and are deleted on request. Analytics data is aggregate and not tied to an individual, so there is nothing held about you personally to delete on that side.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you can ask to see what data is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to how it's used. Email butlen01@outlook.com for any of this. You can also complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), if you believe your data has been mishandled.

Security

This site is served entirely over HTTPS, so data submitted through the contact form is encrypted in transit.

Changes to This Policy

If what this site collects or how it's used changes, this page will be updated to reflect that.
