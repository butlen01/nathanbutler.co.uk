How I Work

Systems and structure, not symptoms.

01

Diagnose before I intervene

I work out whether a performance issue sits in execution, incentives or operating design — then fix the level that actually moves it.

02

Deliver through influence

Much of what I achieve runs through people and functions outside my reporting line — by setting the model and targets they own, not issuing instructions.

03

Decisive, and fair with it

I make the hard structural calls — replacing pay frameworks, exiting unprofitable contracts — while keeping change cost-neutral and redeploying people rather than discarding them.

04

Influence across the matrix

I hold real influence across a complex leadership matrix, trusted by finance, legal, marketing, HR, financial services and estate agency leaders alike. My role has rarely been to command these functions, but to align them, resolving competing priorities and keeping every discipline pulling toward the same commercial outcome.

05

An extra 1% every day

Built a CRM operating system on a simple belief, that an extra one percent every day compounds into results no single heroic effort can match. It created a layer of governance, a shared cadence of pipeline reviews and reporting, and made performance visible at every level. Individual productivity stopped being a matter of instinct and became something to coach and improve, one conversation at a time.

06

Systems that compound client value

I built the structures that let clients draw on the full weight of the group, not just a single service line. By connecting capabilities that usually sit in silos, I lowered acquisition cost, lifted margin on every relationship, and turned one-off instructions into repeat business. The structure did the work quietly in the background, so clients felt a seamless offer rather than a stitched-together one.
