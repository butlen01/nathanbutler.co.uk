Client Case Studies

What I delivered for the accounts themselves.

Winning a mandate is the easy part to talk about. This is what happened next: for the funds, housing associations, councils and developers who depended on the work actually being delivered, and for the people who bought and moved into the homes at the end of it. Anyone can point to a win. The test of a commercial leader is what the client had afterwards, whether the customer was better off, and whether the account was still there at the next tender. A large share of it was regeneration rather than greenfield: a former infirmary, a Victorian hospital, a listed dock warehouse and council-owned land brought forward for homes that were genuinely needed.

c.4,500 units, national accounts 100% account retention, zero accounts lost £1.1bn GDV overseen 250 office delivery network

Public Sector & Institutional Capital

Housing associations, a council-led company and institutional funds, each needing the operation sourced, priced and run, not just a mandate won.

Sanctuary
01 Largest Account by Volume
c.1,500 the largest account in the group by volume

Sanctuary: the largest account in the group, run as one national programme

The largest account in the business, spread the length of the UK, with sales management outside my reporting line and satisfaction scores reported straight to Sanctuary's own board: a standard that wasn't mine to set.

Retained in full through every tender cycle, the position strengthening each round, c.1,500 units won, every site team trained to the board's own standard rather than the contract's minimum.
WV Living
02 Local Authority
6 Sites Hampton Park to Daisy Bank

WV Living: translating council bureaucracy into a buyer's journey

Wolverhampton City Council set up WV Living to deliver genuinely needed homes; whether a council-led company could sell like a commercial developer, across shared ownership, bulk sale and the open market, was the open question.

Six sites sold across three routes to market, bulk sale de-risking the programme before a single owner-occupier moved in, every buyer treated the same regardless of route.
Abbey Place: CBRE Global Investment Management
03 Fractional COO
650+ units across eight sites nationally

Abbey Place: the flagship of CBRE's national fund portfolio

CBRE Global Investment Management buys housing at a discount and holds it as a high-yielding, grant-topped asset. My role wasn't a sales mandate: closer to a fractional Chief Operating Officer, sourcing and advising before a single unit was priced.

650-plus units across eight sites, every scheme performing against the model it was underwritten on, priced against what buyers could actually sustain rather than what the model hoped they could: why Bletchley sold out before a flat was finished.
Octopus Real Estate
04 New Entrant
Full Lifecycle brought in pre-launch, sites nationwide

Octopus Real Estate: the sales and compliance operation, built from scratch

Octopus Real Estate, the property investment arm of Octopus Energy, buys developments and converts them to shared ownership. Sites run the length of the country and I was brought in before any of it was sales-ready.

Account retained on the same terms as the group's most established clients, income caps and buyer priority applied correctly before Homes England ever asked, on a national portfolio built from a blank page.
Landmark Wins

The pitches and regenerations that became the account's calling card.

The Victoria, Glasgow
05 Landmark Win
413 Homes full masterplan for the former Victoria Infirmary

The Victoria, Glasgow: winning the anchor that turned a region around

A failing Scottish unit needed proof it deserved a client's most significant site: the former Victoria Infirmary, a 413-home Glasgow redevelopment won against Savills.

The group's pitch win of the year, phase one run at breakeven by design and renegotiated once proven, 135 affordable homes keeping the former hospital's Nightingale wards for a site Langside had a century of attachment to.
The Tobacco Warehouse, Liverpool
06 7-Year Pursuit
500 units one of the largest single instructions won

The Tobacco Warehouse: winning the world's largest brick building after seven years

The world's largest brick-built building, Liverpool's Stanley Dock Tobacco Warehouse, was the single largest instruction the business had ever had the chance to win: seven years staying in the room before a signed instruction.

The single largest mandate the business has landed, 500-plus apartments sold, two entirely different buyer journeys run side by side: overseas investors buying a landmark, domestic buyers relying on government schemes to get there.
Barnes Village, Cheadle
07 Heritage Regeneration
£1.35m 156 homes, Grade II listed

Barnes Village, Cheadle: building a premium brand a London developer couldn't run alone

Henley Homes, a London developer, bought Barnes Hospital, a Grade II listed Victorian landmark, to convert into 156 homes. What the London-based team couldn't deliver at arm's length was the immaculate, design-led local intensity the premium brand demanded.

£1.35m of fee value at price points South Manchester hadn't seen on a comparable heritage conversion, held to a genuinely immaculate standard throughout because the building's history was the product being sold.
