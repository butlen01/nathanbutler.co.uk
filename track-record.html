Track Record

Six problems. What was actually done about them.

How the business itself was rebuilt — its incentives, its systems, its structure — told through six of the problems it was rebuilt to solve.

×4 divisional revenue £1.1bn GDV overseen c.2,500 units, national accounts 250 office delivery network
Leadership & Change

From recovery to sustained growth

01 Turnaround
35% uplift in new-business wins, year on year

Turning a stock decline into 35% growth — cost-neutrally

Situation

A decline in stock holding was being treated as an execution problem. It wasn't — it was an incentive problem. The pay model in place rewarded the wrong behaviour, and no amount of pushing the team harder was going to fix that.

Approach

Redesigned a long-standing pay framework around a "Win:Sell" model: one team winning instructions, one team selling them, on jointly owned targets. The change was cost-neutral and no one was worse off — the model redirected incentive spend rather than cutting it.

Result

New-business wins rose 35% year on year. Sales recovered from a year-on-year decline to growth — in a contracting market, not a rising one.

02 Rescue
Loss → Profit a unit earmarked for closure

Rescuing a business earmarked for closure

Situation

A Scottish business unit was six figures loss-making and earmarked for closure, its contract base built on unprofitable referral arrangements it couldn't sustain.

Approach

Begun as Residential Development Director: reshaped the contract base away from the unprofitable referrals, restructured the leadership, and redeployed the team cost-neutrally to halt the losses. Then won The Victoria — the regeneration of the former Victoria Hospital in Glasgow — as the anchor instruction, purposefully displacing a major competitor and becoming the group's pitch win of the year.

Result

Completed as Divisional Director: stabilised, then repriced fees from strength as agent of choice, having carried the sales costs to win the account back from weakness. The unit became one of the division's stronger performers, and the recovery earned promotion to Divisional Director.

03 Systems
£1.4m year-on-year rise in new-business wins, Q1

Building the CRM as a growth and governance platform

Situation

Reporting was manual, AML compliance checks were slow and inconsistent, and there was no live view of the sales pipeline to manage performance by.

Approach

Designed and built the group CRM from nothing, as both a growth tool and a governance platform: automated AML compliance, live management information in place of manual reporting, and every field feeding directly into the performance and pipeline cadence. Negotiated the software commercials personally, at purchase and at renewal.

Result

A £1.4m year-on-year rise in new-business wins in the first full quarter, with compliance and reporting now running on systems rather than manual effort.
