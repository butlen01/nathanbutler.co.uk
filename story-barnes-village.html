Barnes Village, Cheadle

Barnes Village — a Grade II Listed Hospital Site Turned 156 Homes

The former Barnes Hospital in Cheadle, Grade II listed and disused, needed a route to market that respected its heritage status while making 156 homes genuinely sellable.

£1.35m 156 homes, Grade II listed

Situation

The former Barnes Hospital in Cheadle, Grade II listed and disused, needed a route to market that respected its heritage status while making 156 homes genuinely sellable.

Approach

Built the go-to-market strategy around the site's heritage rather than around it, positioning the listed building's history as the sale rather than a constraint the sale had to work past.

Result

£1.35m of business secured and 156 homes sold on a site most agencies would have treated as a liability, not an asset.
