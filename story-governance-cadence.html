Governance

An extra 1%, every day

How building the group CRM from nothing turned reporting, compliance and coaching into a system that keeps producing — instead of a fix that needed repeating.

£1.4m year-on-year rise in new-business wins, Q1

Situation

Reporting was manual, AML compliance checks were slow and inconsistent, and there was no live view of the sales pipeline to manage performance by. Improvement depended on whoever happened to notice a problem — a manager spotting a slipping deal, a compliance gap caught by chance — rather than on a system built to surface it automatically.

Approach

  • Designed and built the group CRM from nothing, treating it as both a growth tool and a governance platform rather than two separate systems.
  • Automated AML compliance checks and replaced manual reporting with live management information, so every deal fed directly into the numbers rather than into a spreadsheet someone had to update.
  • Built a weekly coaching and pipeline cadence around the live data, so the same system that produced compliance and reporting also drove the daily coaching conversations.
  • Used that cadence to find the specific touch points where small, daily improvements compounded — an extra 1%, repeated consistently, rather than chasing one large fix that would fade once attention moved elsewhere.
  • Negotiated the software commercials personally, at purchase and at every renewal, keeping the platform's cost aligned to what it was actually delivering.

Result

A £1.4m year-on-year rise in new-business wins in the first full quarter, with compliance and reporting now running on systems rather than manual effort. The same cadence still drives coaching today — a structure that keeps producing, not a one-off intervention.
