Endorsements

Not my words. Theirs.

Four people who worked alongside me, at different points and in different parts of the business, were asked for a plain answer to a plain question: what was it actually like. This is what they said, in full, not trimmed into a single line. Identified by role and how long we worked together, at each person's own preference.

Managing Director Line manager, 10 years working together

Nathan has consistently demonstrated exceptional leadership over a number of years, earning the respect of colleagues, stakeholders and B2B clients alike through his professionalism, integrity and commitment to achieving positive outcomes.

A particularly strong aspect of Nathan's leadership has been his ability to recruit, develop and retain talented people, while creating an environment in which individuals are encouraged to take ownership of their own performance and development. He has successfully recruited into the business, often within challenging budgetary parameters, and has supported colleagues through different stages of their careers, including progression into senior management and director-level roles. He has also shown a consistent ability to recognise individual strengths and create opportunities for people to broaden their experience, including moving colleagues into roles with greater strategic or commercial responsibility.

His approach to people development is based on providing clarity around expectations and responsibilities, establishing appropriate parameters within which individuals can operate, and then giving people the autonomy to deliver. Rather than creating dependency on management direction, Nathan seeks to empower individuals to take responsibility for their own outcomes, intervening where necessary and managing by exception. This has helped create teams that are more confident, accountable and capable of performing independently, while providing a strong pipeline of talent for the future.

One example that stands out is how Nathan helped develop and oversee a significant change to the variable pay structure for our delivery teams, a critical initiative that affected a large number of colleagues and required communicating potentially difficult messages about individual remuneration. He approached it with professionalism, clarity and integrity throughout, quickly establishing credibility by ensuring the rationale for the changes was clearly understood, while remaining focused on a fair and consistent outcome for everyone affected. He demonstrated real emotional intelligence and resilience, addressing concerns openly and constructively while maintaining momentum and confidence in the process. The result was an exceptionally well-executed transition: the changes were implemented successfully, colleague concerns were effectively managed, and the business was left with a significantly more sustainable and effective remuneration model.

Another example is the development of a strategic B2B relationship that began as a local opportunity and was ultimately expanded into a national partnership. The company, now a national client with multiple income streams, had previously sat on the periphery of our business with no obvious route to a meaningful relationship. Through his ability to establish credibility, understand stakeholder needs, build trust and identify mutually beneficial opportunities, Nathan grew that relationship far beyond its original scope, creating multiple revenue streams and positioning us as a trusted strategic partner. That work resulted in a national partnership expected to generate revenue in the region of £2 million over eighteen months, while also enhancing our reputation within the sector.

Nathan also made a significant contribution to organisational performance through his involvement in implementing a new CRM platform across ten business units within the Group. Working closely with internal IT, Group stakeholders and external delivery partners, he played a pivotal role in ensuring the system that was delivered met the actual operational requirements of the business rather than simply the initial assumptions of the implementation teams, balancing the needs of end users against the strategic objectives of the organisation throughout. He also worked extensively with leadership teams and operational colleagues to drive understanding, engagement and adoption, not just implementing a new system but building the understanding, confidence and accountability needed for colleagues to use it effectively. Despite the scale and complexity of the change, the project was delivered successfully across all ten business units, leaving the Group with improved visibility, greater consistency of process and a stronger foundation for best practice.

Nathan has an outstanding foundation of management and leadership experience, and he already displays many of the qualities of a truly exceptional operator. Any organisation would benefit significantly from what he brings.

Director 15 years competing and working together

I first came to know Nathan as a competitor, before I joined the business he was leading at Countrywide. He very much stood out as one of the strongest and most formidable operators I had encountered: relentless, commercially astute and genuinely difficult to beat. There were a number of canal-side developments in Manchester, and others across the Northwest region, that I pitched for and graciously lost out to Nathan. I developed enormous respect for him long before we worked together. That is not praise I give lightly to a competitor; Nathan earned it through the consistency of his performance, the strength of his leadership and the exceptionally high standards he set.

When the opportunity arose to work with Nathan, the high opinion I had formed of him did not simply hold up at close quarters, it grew considerably. He is decisive without ever being reckless, able to make difficult calls quickly while explaining his reasoning and being completely straight with people, even when the message was challenging. When I joined, the responsibilities of the role I was recruited for changed and grew, but Nathan fully explained this to me, allowed me to make an informed choice, and supported me all the way. He gives his people genuine ownership and backs them wholeheartedly to deliver; I always felt trusted, supported and accountable but never micromanaged. Just as importantly, he never allowed the pressure of hitting a target number to override what was right for the customer or the long-term interests of the business. His combination of commercial drive, sound judgement, integrity and genuine belief in his team is exceptionally rare, and it makes him an outstanding leader.

Going from competing against someone to actively choosing to work for them I believe says more than most references ever could. I would make that decision again without hesitation. I recommend Nathan with enthusiasm and without reservation. Any business would be exceptionally fortunate to benefit from his leadership, integrity, energy and judgement, and I could not endorse him more strongly.

Operations & Compliance Manager 7 years working together in finance & operations

I worked with Nathan for seven years, first as the B2B Finance Business Partner embedded in his P&L, and later as Operations Manager in his team. In all that time I don't think we had a quiet quarter, and that was very much down to him.

Nathan has a restless commercial curiosity. He is never satisfied with a number on its own: he wants to know what is moving it, why, and what to do about it before the next reporting cycle arrives.

Much of what we built together started as analysis I brought him, covering conversion rates, cost bases, margin by scheme and whatever else the business needed visibility on that quarter. What set Nathan apart was that he never treated analysis as a report to file away. If I showed him a trend, his first question was what we were going to do about it, and his second was whether it had worked. He pushed me past the headline figure to the second- and third-order explanations underneath, and more than once a piece of work I thought was finished became the start of a completely different conversation.

Sitting that close to his P&L, I saw how precisely he understood which levers move the result and which only feel like they should. He could tell you without hesitation whether a problem sat in pricing, cost, mix or volume, and he never reached for the easy lever when it was the wrong one. That is how he held margin through a difficult market without resorting to indiscriminate cost-cutting.

When I moved into his team as Operations Manager, I saw the other side of it: he created a culture where people could bring bad numbers early, because he cared more about understanding the problem than finding someone to blame for it.

I've worked with plenty of commercially minded operators. Very few understand their numbers as deeply as Nathan, and fewer still welcome being challenged on them the way he does. Any organisation serious about commercial rigour would be fortunate to have him.

Group Research Consultancy Director Two spells, 11 years working together

When I look back at how the research side of the business changed, the shift wasn't really about the analysis itself. It was about how we thought about what we'd built. We'd been producing market research reports purely for internal use for years. It was good work, but nobody had ever asked whether it had value outside the building. Nathan was the one who looked at what the team was already producing and saw something we didn't: that developers would actually pay for it.

That's easy to say in hindsight, but at the time it meant changing how the whole function operated, not just the pricing. It meant taking the report from a statistical snapshot to a tangible, dynamic document that could tell a specific story based on individual project circumstances. The work we completed with Stoke Council, Muse (Morgan Sindall) and Wolverhampton Council was what really started to take the strategy of operating for profit in a different direction.

Nathan sat between the analysis and the commercial conversation in a way I hadn't seen before: technically we knew the work was correct and meaningful, but it took him to translate that into something a developer would actually act on and pay for. We wanted our insight to translate immediately into the thinking of our clients, so we understood their requirements first time.

What stood out to me wasn't the detail, since that was always our job. It was the pace and the direction. He set a deadline and expected it to be hit, pushed the team to reshape the format more than once when the first version didn't land commercially, and kept the whole thing moving in a way that meant it travelled rather than sat as a good idea nobody acted on.

The more we challenged ourselves, and got under the skin of clients, the further it went: we were billing up to £15,000 per report, and we even secured clients who paid for smaller, repeat reports, creating recurring revenue.

At a later date, when the commercial strategy was expanded company-wide, Nathan worked with me on integrating our business into the CRM rollout, and we were able to follow the customer journey between the client and the business development team. The results were profound: conversion from pitch to mandate secured increased, and total contracted value where a report was used as leverage sat at over £3m in a year.

Nathan was instrumental in training the team to use the data and build mature conversations with clients that helped us stand out from a crowded field. He was also excellent at understanding when to leverage a report for cash versus offering it ex gratia, and more importantly, we built systems to control that decision through the team rather than leaving it to individual judgement.

I'd say without hesitation that the research and marketing functions became income lines, and a significantly positive influence on new business procurement, because someone from outside the function forced a different way of thinking about them. That person was Nathan.
