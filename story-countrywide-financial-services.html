Company Case Study — Countrywide Financial Services

Building an Income Stream Into Every Sale

Financial services sat outside the business entirely, run through third-party providers with nothing coming back to the division doing the selling. This is how Countrywide Financial Services was integrated directly into the new-homes sales process instead.

+£560 average added per sale, at close to zero marginal cost

Situation

Every new-homes buyer needed a mortgage, and most needed protection products alongside it — demand that already existed, on every single sale. None of it generated a return for the business creating that demand. It was referred away to third-party providers as a courtesy, a line that only ever cost time and cost money, in a business built entirely around the sale it was attached to.

Approach

Engaged Countrywide Financial Services directly to build a bespoke arrangement, rather than treating financial services as a referral bolted on after the sale was already done. The offer was integrated into the sales process itself — raised at the point a buyer was already committing, not chased afterwards — and structured so the business accrued a share of the incremental income it created.

Result

An average of £560 added to the P&L per sale, at close to zero marginal cost, since the demand already existed and the only change was capturing it rather than giving it away. One of the cleanest routes to a higher return on sale available, on a product that used to cost the business money just to refer.
