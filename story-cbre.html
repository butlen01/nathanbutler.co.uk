Abbey Place — Running the Full CBRE Fund Mandate

Abbey Place, held by CBRE Global Investment Management as an institutional fund, meant sales and marketing execution had to sit inside strict compliance and capital funding guidelines, not run alongside them as a separate concern.

Full Mandate sales, marketing, compliance and funding, one team

Situation

Abbey Place, held by CBRE Global Investment Management as an institutional fund, meant sales and marketing execution had to sit inside strict compliance and capital funding guidelines, not run alongside them as a separate concern.

Approach

Took on the full mandate on the fund's behalf: sales, marketing, and the compliance and capital funding requirements that came with institutional money, built into the delivery rather than checked after the fact.

Result

A fund client able to hand over sales and marketing in full, confident that compliance and capital funding requirements were being met without needing to check.
