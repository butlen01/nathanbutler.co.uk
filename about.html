About

Twenty years. One pattern.

I started as a school-leaver trainee negotiator, studying for a construction diploma and then a building surveying degree alongside full-time work. Every role since has followed the same shape: take on something underperforming and leave it stronger than I found it. A branch, then a region, then a business unit, then a division, then five of them. The scale changed. The job did not.

What I Am Looking For

A remit, not just a role.

A commercial or operational leadership role with a genuine remit: a P&L, a team, and the authority to change how the business works rather than only how hard it works. Commercial Director, Sales Director, Chief Operating Officer or Country Manager.

Sector matters less to me than whether the commercial fundamentals are recognisable: a client to win, a promise to deliver, a customer at the end of it, and a number to hold. I have spent my career in property and I would be glad to stay in it, but nothing on this site depends on the sector. Based in Chester, working nationally.

The Journey

From trainee negotiator to Divisional Director.

2002

Trainee Negotiator, Beresford Adams

Joined the industry as a school leaver, studying full-time for a National Diploma in Construction alongside the job. Progressed to Senior Sales Negotiator and then Residential Valuer, the youngest in the group, with the highest average fee per transaction in the region for two years running.

2004 – 2008

BSc Building Surveying, Liverpool John Moores University

Completed a full surveying degree while building a career in residential sales, the technical foundation underneath everything that followed in development and land.

2008

Sequence (UK) Ltd

Took a loss-making Northwich branch with a nil pipeline and made it the most profitable in its cluster within six months. Promoted to Senior Manager in Chester, then Regional Sales Manager across five Wirral offices, preventing three branch closures along the way.

2013 – 2019

Four promotions in six years

Joined Countrywide's residential development business and moved through Business Development Manager, New Homes Director and Residential Development Director, each role widening the remit from a single patch to a multi-site, multi-team operation, and each one earned by turning around what came before it.

2019 – 2026

Divisional Director

Seven years leading a dispersed, multi-division P&L: five divisions and a national affordable housing operation, c.£1.1bn GDV, more than fourfold revenue growth, and a business unit rescued from closure along the way.
